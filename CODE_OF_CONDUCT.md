# Code of Conduct

Sakshi is built in the open by people with different backgrounds, experience and
opinions. We want everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests,
discussions or anywhere else in the project, to be able to do so without
harassment or hostility.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent, especially in disagreement.
- Critique ideas and code, never people.
- Be patient with newcomers and with people writing in a second language.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and give it constructively.
- Own your mistakes and help fix them.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, intimidation, threats or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainers
privately through the repository's private reporting channel (see
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)), marking the report as a conduct issue. Reports are
handled confidentially. Please include links or screenshots where you can.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues that
break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone whose
behaviour they judge harmful to the community. Decisions are made fairly and
explained where that is safe to do.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, and in public spaces when someone is
representing the project.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.
